# Emirates NDSS Framework

## Purpose
Evaluate digital customer interactions against Emirates NDSS.

## Evaluation Principles
- Evidence-based evaluation only.
- Assess chronologically.
- Do not assume actions occurred.
- Do not assume actions failed.
- Use exact NDSS criteria.
- Use statuses: Met, Improvement Required, Not Applicable, Insufficient Evidence / System Validation Required.

# THE HUMAN [UNDERSTANDING]
## Opening
- Acknowledges chatbot information.
- Refers to previous interactions where applicable.
- Uses customer name appropriately.
## Empathy
- Understanding, care, compassion, positive tone.
## Customer Recognition
- Personalised and context-relevant recognition.
## Active Listening
- No unnecessary repeated questions.
- Multi-part queries addressed.
- Appropriate probing questions.
## Efficient Messaging & Writing Style
- Concise.
- Conversational.
- Personalised templates.
- Proper grammar.
- Appropriate emojis.
## Closing
- Relevant and emotionally appropriate.
